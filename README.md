# AppBox

Tired of maintaining multiple side projects and personal tools? Yeah, me too! \
AppBox is basically a super-app where I dump all my small applications and utilities for easy access and management.

## Sub Apps

- **Wallmatic**: Automatically changes your wallpaper with time. Set it and enjoy fresh backgrounds throughout the day.
- **Topty**: A lightweight authenticator app for managing your 2FA codes securely and efficiently.
- **2048**: The classic 2048 puzzle game. Challenge yourself and reach the 2048 tile!
- **Jest**: Get your daily dose of humor with curated jokes and fun content.
